# Changelog

## 0.1.1 — contract corrections and support-provider receiving

- Recheck mandatory runtime findings before terminal completion, preserving failed,
  unresolved and waiting-human outcomes even when a recipe catches their exceptions (#15).
- Distinguish declared human review from a missing executable checker in definition
  audits, without inventing review evidence or scientific admission (#16).
- Admit ArgDigest 0.17, SMonitor 0.22 and PyUnitWizard 0.29; qualify public Recorda
  0.3.3 recording and Ackredit attribution with the new cohort while preserving
  legacy installed-test coverage (#14).
- Reconcile the completed MOLI registry synchronization under #9.

The [source qualification record](devguide/releases/0.1.1.md) distinguishes acceptance
of this patch from package publication and scientific validation. API/storage remain
experimental. The frozen 0.1.0 tag and its original files are unchanged.

## 0.1.0 — experimental local milestone

First experimental local Praxis implementation:

- Versioned Capability/Protocol catalog, explicit scientific admission and scoped
  assessment/suitability histories; documentation of contracts, tradeoffs and resource profiles.
- Recorded selection and exact implementation binding; typed quantities and parameters;
  pre/runtime/post guarantees, human evidence, bounded branches/repetitions and child methods.
- Retained attempt/check/step records, independent audit, offline trace and replay preflight.
- Physical benchmark metrics, interruption checkpoints, descriptive comparison and
  reanalysis of retained outputs; Markdown and HTML reports.
- Optional borrowed Recorda operation recording and Ackredit attribution sessions.
- Filled hydrogen-refinement proposals and real public MolSysMT RMSD example.

Release qualification and tag status are recorded in [Praxis #9](https://github.com/uibcdf/praxis/issues/9).
This milestone does not promise API/storage compatibility for subsequent
0.x releases or scientific validation. Hydrogen adapters, shared MOLI invocation,
parallel scheduling, durable suspension/resume and rollback remain outside this milestone.
Public distribution, supported-platform claims and archival status depend on the
[release acceptance record](devguide/releases/0.1.0.md), not on this entry.
