# Design Boundaries

1. Capability ≠ Protocol.
2. Praxis know-how ≠ project-specific Discovery state.
3. Protocol orchestration ≠ ownership of underlying scientific APIs.
4. AI-generated methodology ≠ validated methodology.
5. Successful Run ≠ automatic Capability promotion.
6. Validation, limitations, applicability, provenance, and versioning are first-class.
7. Protocol selection may be deterministic when explicit policy is sufficient; scientific judgment remains with humans and/or MOLI Agent.
8. Important persistent objects should be serializable and referencable in accordance with MOLI Architecture 1.0.
9. Local-first implementation must not prevent future shared/remote deployment.
